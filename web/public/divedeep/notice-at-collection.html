Notice at Collection

This notice explains the categories of information DiveDeep expects to collect at or before the point of collection.

Account and transaction information

We collect name, email address, authentication information, selected service level, billing status, and account events to create and secure the account, provide the selected service, prevent abuse, and maintain transaction records.

Protected-profile information

After authorization, a customer may provide names, aliases, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, usernames, owned domains, profile URLs, reference images, and jurisdiction information. We use these details to locate likely exposures, distinguish the customer from other people, prepare supported requests, and report results.

Authorization and technical evidence

When the customer signs the Limited Authorization, we record the signed document, document version, typed signature, date and time, authenticated session reference, browser or device information, and the server-observed network address. These fields establish the scope and evidence of consent, support security, and help answer recipient verification questions.

Evidence and request records

We may collect public URLs, screenshots, hashes, source details, correspondence, request status, recipient responses, and verification results. Sensitive evidence is access-restricted and displayed only when necessary.

Sharing

We disclose only the identifiers and supporting information reasonably needed by a removal target, service provider, payment provider, security provider, or legal recipient for the stated service purpose. We do not sell a customer’s removal profile or use it to build advertising audiences.

Retention

Retention depends on account status, request status, security obligations, dispute needs, and the type of record. Customers may request account closure or exercise applicable privacy rights through the methods described in the Privacy Rights page.
