Security overview

Security and Retention

This overview describes protective practices without exposing operational details that would weaken the service.

Data protection

DiveDeep is designed to encrypt sensitive information at rest and in transit, isolate private evidence from public files, protect authenticated sessions, validate uploaded evidence, and avoid returning stored credentials to browsers or logs.

Access control

Administrative access is role-restricted. High-sensitivity evidence and reference-image access should require elevated authority and produce an audit event. Customer sessions may access only records belonging to that customer.

Integrity and auditability

Executed authorizations are stored as immutable document snapshots with SHA-256 integrity values. Authorization events use linked event hashes. Request and evidence records preserve references needed to explain source, status, approval, and transmission.

Retention categories

Account and subscription records, authorization records, request history, evidence, support data, security logs, and backups may follow different schedules. Evidence should be removed when no longer operationally necessary; executed authorization and transmitted-request history may be retained longer for proof, security, and dispute needs.

Incident response

DiveDeep should maintain procedures to detect, contain, investigate, document, remediate, and communicate material security incidents. Notice obligations depend on the affected information and applicable law.

Reporting concerns

Report suspected account compromise, exposed evidence, or security concerns through the designated security or privacy contact. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive documents in an ordinary email.
