Privacy Policy

This policy describes how OSINT Dive Deep v1 and the entity identified in the customer’s order confirmation (“DiveDeep,” “we,” or “us”) handle information used by the service.

Information we collect

We collect account information, transaction and subscription records, protected-profile identifiers, connected-account details approved by the customer, reference images, public exposure evidence, request correspondence, authorization records, support communications, security logs, and device or network metadata.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate customers, operate authorized discovery, confirm likely matches, prepare or deliver supported privacy and removal requests, receive responses, verify outcomes, maintain audit records, prevent abuse, secure the service, provide support, comply with law, and improve reliability without repurposing protected-profile data for advertising.

How we disclose information

Information may be disclosed to the specific website, broker, platform, publisher, host, search provider, or other recipient involved in an authorized request; to service providers that support hosting, security, communications, billing, or document delivery; or when required to protect rights, investigate abuse, or comply with law. We limit each disclosure to the information reasonably necessary for that purpose.

Sensitive information

Reference images, identity documents, sensitive-site evidence, precise contact details, authorization records, and technical signing evidence receive restricted access. DiveDeep does not require identity documents by default. A recipient-controlled verification process may require the customer to respond directly.

Retention and deletion

We retain records according to their operational purpose. Active authorization and request records remain available while needed to deliver and document the service. Staged or duplicate evidence should be removed on shorter schedules. Security, transaction, dispute, and legal records may be retained after account closure when reasonably necessary.

Customer choices

Customers can update protected information, disconnect supported accounts, download or revoke authorization, request access or correction, request account closure, and contact us regarding deletion or appeal. Revocation stops new submissions but does not erase the fact that prior requests were authorized and transmitted.

Security

DiveDeep uses encryption, access controls, session protection, audit logs, restricted evidence handling, and integrity records. No system can guarantee absolute security. Customers should use unique passwords and report suspected account misuse promptly.

Children and representatives

The standard self-service authorization is for adults signing for themselves. A parent, guardian, estate representative, or other legal representative requires a separate verification and authorization process.

Contact

Privacy questions and rights requests may be sent to privacy@divedeep.example.invalid. Written notices may also be sent to the business address identified in the customer’s order confirmation or account records.
