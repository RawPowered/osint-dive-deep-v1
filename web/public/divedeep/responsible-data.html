Our operating commitments

Responsible Data Use

A privacy service should not create a second uncontrolled profile of the person it is trying to protect.

Purpose limitation

We use protected-profile information only to authenticate the customer, locate relevant public exposure, distinguish likely matches, conduct authorized request activity, verify results, secure the service, and satisfy legal or audit obligations.

Data minimization

We collect progressively. Basic account information comes first. Additional identifiers, reference images, or verification material are requested only when they materially improve matching or are required by a recipient. We do not require identity documents for ordinary onboarding.

Minimum necessary disclosure

Each request should disclose the smallest set of identifiers and evidence reasonably necessary for that recipient to identify the record and process the request.

Prohibited internal uses

We do not sell protected-profile information, create advertising audiences from removal data, train public models on customer evidence, publish customer evidence, or use an active authorization outside its stated scope.

Restricted access

Access is limited by role and operational need. Sensitive evidence, signing metadata, and customer reference images should not be available to ordinary support or analytics users.

Human review and accountability

Uncertain matches require review. High-sensitivity findings remain hidden by default. Administrative actions and authorization changes are recorded so the service can explain who did what and when.

Retention and disposal

Duplicate, staged, expired, and no-longer-needed evidence should be removed on defined schedules. Executed authorization and request history may be retained longer to prove scope, transmission, revocation, security, and dispute handling.
