Authorized-Agent Policy

DiveDeep acts only within a customer’s active, signed, and narrowly defined authorization.

What DiveDeep may do

Depending on the selected service and recipient process, DiveDeep may submit supported access, correction, deletion, suppression, opt-out, de-indexing, image-removal, or content-removal requests; communicate with recipients; provide minimum matching information; receive status messages; follow up; and document outcomes.

What DiveDeep may not do

The standard authorization does not permit DiveDeep to access financial, medical, credit, or private communications accounts; commence litigation; settle claims; waive rights; accept money; sign sworn statements; change passwords; or impersonate the customer outside the limited request process.

Recipient verification

A recipient may ask for the signed authorization, contact the customer directly, require account login, or require additional identity verification. DiveDeep will not bypass captcha, authentication, access controls, or recipient-controlled identity checks.

Duration

The dashboard displays the effective date and expiration date. DiveDeep may require renewal when the authorization expires, when the scope changes materially, or when a recipient requires a newer record.

Revocation

The customer may revoke from the dashboard or through the privacy contact. Revocation blocks new submissions. DiveDeep may preserve the executed record and prior request history to document that earlier activity was authorized.

Representatives

The self-service form covers an adult signing for themselves. Parents, guardians, estate representatives, attorneys, and other agents require a separate process and supporting authority.
