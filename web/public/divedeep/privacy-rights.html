Privacy Rights and Requests

DiveDeep provides account controls and a direct privacy channel for questions about information held by DiveDeep itself.

Available requests

Subject to applicable law and verification, a customer may request access, correction, deletion, account closure, a portable copy, information about disclosure, restriction of certain processing, or review of a prior decision.

Authorization controls

The customer can download the signed Limited Authorization, view its scope and expiration, and revoke it in the dashboard. Revocation is not the same as deleting the account or canceling billing.

Verification

We verify requests in a manner proportionate to the sensitivity of the record. We may use the authenticated account, confirmed email, transaction history, signed authorization, or other appropriate information. We will not disclose sensitive records when identity cannot be reasonably verified.

Authorized representatives

A person acting for a customer must provide sufficient proof of authority. DiveDeep may also contact the customer directly when permitted or required.

Appeals and complaints

If DiveDeep denies or limits a rights request, the response should explain the reason and available appeal or complaint channel when applicable.

How to submit

Use the account support channel or email privacy@divedeep.example.invalid. Include the account email and the request being made. Do not email identity documents unless DiveDeep specifically requests them through an approved secure channel.
