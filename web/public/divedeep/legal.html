Formal authorization is separate from signup

Creating an account does not authorize DiveDeep to contact websites, data brokers, publishers, platforms, hosts, or search providers for you. After signup, the account holder must complete a separate Limited Authorization and Consent form. Removal activity remains blocked until that record is active.

Customer control

The customer can review the current authorization, download the executed record, see its expiration date, and revoke it from the account dashboard. Revocation blocks new submissions.
