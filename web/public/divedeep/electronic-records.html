Electronic delivery disclosure

Electronic Records and Signatures Disclosure

This disclosure applies when a customer elects to execute and receive DiveDeep records electronically.

Records covered

Electronic records may include the Limited Authorization, consent receipts, terms, policies, notices, account communications, request status, billing records, and support messages.

Consent and intent

On the authorization screen, the customer must separately affirm consent to electronic records and intent to sign. Selecting the final authorization button applies the typed signature to the displayed record.

Technical requirements

The customer needs a current web browser, internet access, an active email address, and the ability to download, store, or print HTML or PDF records. The executed authorization can be downloaded from the dashboard.

Paper copies

A customer may print the downloaded record or request a paper copy through the privacy contact. A reasonable delivery fee may apply only if disclosed before the request is completed.

Withdrawal

The customer may withdraw consent to electronic delivery by contacting DiveDeep. Withdrawal may prevent online execution or continuation of features that require electronic records. Withdrawal does not invalidate records already signed or delivered electronically.

Updating contact information

The customer is responsible for maintaining an accessible email address and current account information.
